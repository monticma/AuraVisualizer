## Open the 3D Visualizer

Aura analyzes your workspace and renders an interactive 3D dependency graph.

**How to open:**
- Press `Ctrl+Shift+A` (`Cmd+Shift+A` on Mac)
- Or click the **Aura** icon in the Activity Bar
- Or run the command: *Aura: Visualize Codebase*

The analysis runs in a background thread — your editor stays responsive.
